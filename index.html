Turn M-Pesa PDF statements into Excel

Add one or more Safaricom M-Pesa statements. You get a formatted workbook with every transaction, who it went to, money in and money out listed line by line, monthly totals, charges, Fuliza and a running-balance check. The PDF is read inside this browser and never uploaded.

Only used to open the PDF here. Leave blank if the file isn't locked.

    Statement

    Ask about your statement

    Answered in this browser from the transactions above, by calculation. Nothing is sent anywhere, and every answer lists the transactions behind it. Ask a question, or look up particular transactions by a name, a phone, till, paybill or account number, a receipt code, an amount or a date.

    Private by design. Your statement is read with pdf.js inside this page. Nothing from it is sent to a server or kept after you close the tab.
    Works with Safaricom M-Pesa customer statements downloaded as PDF from the M-Pesa app or USSD (*334#). Scanned or photographed statements have no text layer and can't be read.
    On a phone, choose the PDF from Files, Downloads or your email app. If the file picker does not open inside another app, open this page in Chrome or Safari.
    How it checks itself: every row's balance must equal the previous balance plus money in minus money out. Rows that don't follow are flagged in the workbook, and totals are compared with the statement's own summary.
    Code and tests: github.com/maidenpeter/mpesa-statement-to-excel. The tests run the page on synthetic statements and fail if any request leaves this page.
